## Movie Spotlight: Inside Out

*Inside Out* is an animated movie about Riley, a girl adjusting to a new city. The story follows the emotions that help her make sense of what she is going through.

### What stood out to me

- **Joy** wants Riley to stay happy, even when the move gets difficult.
- **Sadness** helps Riley express what she misses and ask for support.
- The film shows how **empathy** can start with listening to someone.

> My takeaway: It is okay to feel sad, and talking about it can bring people closer.
